## Models downloaded at runtime

The app downloads the models from Hugging Face only when they are used. They are neither part of the repository nor of the release package. Revisions, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `src/LocalDictation/model-catalog.json`.

- Whisper large-v3-turbo © 2022 OpenAI, MIT (`licenses/Whisper-model.txt`), https://github.com/openai/whisper. The app uses the ggml conversion with q5_0 quantization `ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin` from https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp (MIT), revision 5359861c739e955e79d9a303bcbc70fb988958b1.
- Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 © NVIDIA, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3. Modified: the app uses the INT8-quantized ONNX export by csukuangfj (sherpa-onnx) from https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-int8, revision 2bda32ec70b097a55adaa07d9a7173915b43cc78. That repository does not declare a separate license.
